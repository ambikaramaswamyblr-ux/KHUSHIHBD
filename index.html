Dear Khushi, ♡

Happy birthday, my girl. ❤️

I honestly don't know how to write something that fully explains how much a person means to me without making it sound like I'm writing a whole emotional movie script, so please bear with me.

I'm really happy that I have you in my life. I hope you know that. I hope you know that you don't have to do anything extraordinary to deserve love, friendship, or happiness. You deserve those things simply because you're you.

I hope this year brings you so many little moments that make you genuinely happy. The kind of happiness that sneaks up on you when you're laughing at something stupid, listening to a song you love, eating something delicious, reading a really good book, or spending time with the people who feel like home.

I hope you get to do more of the things you love. I hope you become more confident in yourself. I hope you discover new things, meet good people, make beautiful memories, and learn that it's okay when life doesn't go exactly how you imagined.

And on the days when things aren't great, I hope you remember that you don't have to pretend everything is okay around the people who care about you. You can have bad days. You can be confused. You can change your mind. You can take a break. You don't have to be happy every second just because it's your birthday week or because people expect you to have everything together.

I hope you always have people around you who make you feel appreciated, listened to, and understood. People who celebrate your wins without making you feel like you have to earn them. People who can laugh with you one minute and be there for you when you need them the next.

I hope I can be one of those people for you.

Thank you for being part of my life. Thank you for all the conversations, the laughter, the random moments, and the memories we already have. And thank you in advance for all the ridiculous things I'm sure we'll end up laughing about in the future.

Also, please never become so mature that we stop being absolutely ridiculous together. I have a reputation to maintain. And someone needs to keep the Attack on Titan investigation alive. 😭

But really, Khushi, I wish you so much happiness. Not just the big, obvious kind, but the small everyday kind too. I hope you have reasons to smile on ordinary Tuesdays. I hope you feel proud of yourself. I hope you get to look back at this year and think, "You know what? That was a good year."

No matter how much life changes, I hope you never forget that you've made a difference in the lives of the people around you, including mine.

You deserve a birthday full of love, your favourite things, good food, good music, and people who remind you how special you are to them.

So here's to another year of you being you. To more memories, more laughter, more stories, more music, more food, and probably more suspicious conversations about a certain someone. 👀

Happy birthday again, Khushi. I love you, and I'm really glad you're here.

Always rooting for you, ♡
Your bestie.
